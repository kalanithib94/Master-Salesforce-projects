# Test Design: System admin | Outlook | Inbox & Outbox

For each scenario: what you need to run the test, and what you should see from the code (expected result).

---

## Quick Match

| # | Scenario | Needed for testing | Expected from code |
|---|----------|--------------------|--------------------|
| 1 | Inbox - Email from one Contact, one Account, one Opportunity | One Contact with one Account and one Opportunity in Salesforce. One email in inbox From that Contact's email address. Outlook connected. | After you match and import, one EmailMessage is created and linked to that Contact, Account, and Opportunity. No errors. |
| 2 | Select up to 20 emails and click Import selected | Up to 20 emails in inbox; some match Contacts, some do not. Outlook connected. | All emails that match a Contact are imported and linked. Emails with no match stay unmatched. Counts reflect matched and unmatched. No error. |
| 3 | Select more than 20 emails and click Import selected | More than 20 emails selected in email sync UI. | Error toast message: "Too many emails selected". No import. |
| 4 | Already imported emails for rerun | Emails that were already imported in a previous run. Same user, Outlook connected. | Already imported emails cannot be selected for manual match (greyed out or not shown as selectable). No duplicate import. |
| 5 | Already imported emails after deleting logs or staging | Emails were imported earlier; then logs or staging records for that run are deleted. EmailMessage records still exist in Salesforce. | Those emails appear again as available for import. On rerun, no duplicate EmailMessage is created; existing records stay. |
| 6 | Inbox - Email from non-Contact (no Contact or Lead match) | Email in inbox From an address that does not match any Contact or Lead in Salesforce. | Email stays unmatched. Associate button not available or import not possible for that email. No EmailMessage created. |
| 7 | Contact with no direct Account | One Contact with no Account linked. Email in inbox From that Contact's email. | On import, EmailMessage is created and linked only to the Contact. No Account or Opportunity link. |
| 8 | Contact with multiple Accounts and multiple Opportunities | One Contact linked to multiple Accounts and multiple Opportunities. Email From that Contact. | In the match popup you can choose one Account and one Opportunity. After import, EmailMessage is linked only to the selected Contact, Account, and Opportunity. No spill to other Accounts or Opportunities. |
| 9 | Inbox - Forwarded thread emails | Forwarded email thread (multiple messages) in inbox with full body. | All messages in the thread import successfully. Email body is not truncated. EmailMessage(s) linked correctly. |
| 10 | Same email id with multiple Contact, Account, Opportunities | One sender email that exists as multiple Contacts across multiple Accounts and Opportunities. Email in inbox From that address. | You can select one Contact (and Account/Opportunity) in the match popup. After import, EmailMessage is logged only to the selected Contact (and chosen Account/Opportunity). |
| 11 | Contact with one Account, no Opportunity | Contact linked to one Account only; no Opportunity. Email From that Contact. | On import, EmailMessage is logged to the Contact and to the Account. No Opportunity. |
| 12 | Thread emails more than 2 back and forth | Thread in inbox with more than 2 messages (back and forth). | All messages in the thread are imported and linked consistently to the same Contact/Account/Opportunity. Each message creates its own EmailMessage record (logs separately). |
| 13 | Contact 1 and Contact 2 same Account reply | Email from Contact 1; reply from Contact 2 (same Account). Both in inbox. | Both emails import and link to the correct Contact (Contact 1 and Contact 2). Both directions linked to the right Contacts and Account. |
| 14 | Outbox - Send to 2 Contacts in To | Sent email with 2 Contacts in To address. | Both Contacts get matched and imported; EmailMessage logged under both Contact records. |
| 15 | Outbox - Send to 1 Contact in To and 1 in CC | Sent email with 1 Contact in To and 1 Contact in CC. | Both Contacts get matched and imported; EmailMessage logged under both. |
| 16 | Email from Contact to 2 different Users | One email From a Contact sent to 2 different internal users (2 inboxes). | When the first user imports, the email is imported. When the second user opens email sync, that email shows "already imported" and cannot be imported again. No duplicate. |
| 17 | Account with website, no Contact; email from domain-matching address | Account with website/domain; no Contact. Email in inbox From an address whose domain matches the Account website. | Email stays unmatched. No EmailMessage created. No import based on domain only. |
| 18 | Email from non-Contact at same Account | Account has Contact A. Email in inbox From a different person (not a Contact) at the same account/domain. | System matches only by exact Contact email. This sender is not a Contact, so email stays unmatched. No Account match by domain. |
| 19 | Email with attachment - Has file button | Inbox has at least one email with attachment. "Has file" filter/button in UI. | Clicking "Has file" filters and shows only emails that have attachments in that section. |
| 20 | Email with attachments import | Email with one or more attachments. | Import succeeds and EmailMessage is created. Attachment handling (e.g. saving files) is not in scope for this release. |
| 21 | Large email body | Email in inbox with very large body (e.g. long HTML/text). | Email imports successfully. Body is not truncated (or truncation is per defined limits and import still succeeds). |
| 22 | No subject email | Email with empty or missing subject. | Import succeeds. Subject is stored as blank in EmailMessage. |
| 23 | Very long subject | Email with very long subject line. | Import succeeds. Subject is truncated per system limits and stored. |
| 24 | Multiple languages, special characters, non-Latin, emoji | Email with non-Latin text, special characters, emoji in body/subject. | Import succeeds. Content is stored correctly without truncation or corruption. |
| 25 | Email sorting | Inbox and Outbox each have multiple emails. | Email sync page always shows latest email first (inbox and outbox). Sort order is correct after import. |
| 26 | Imported badge and record pills | After import, view the same email in the list. | Imported emails show the imported badge and related record pills (Contact/Account/Opportunity). Clicking a pill opens/redirects to that record. |
| 27 | Notification after successful import | Complete a successful import (Quick Match). | A notification is received when import completes. Clicking the notification takes you to the email message record(s). |
| 28 | Exclude Confidential enabled | Exclude Confidential turned on in settings. Inbox has at least one email marked confidential. | Confidential emails are skipped. Excluded count and summary are correct. No EmailMessage for excluded emails. |
| 29 | Exclude Internal enabled, domain set; internal-only email | Exclude Internal enabled and domain configured. Email in inbox is from internal (same domain). | Email is excluded per rule. Toast: "1 email excluded by exclusion rules. No emails imported". Excluded count correct. |
| 30 | Exclude Internal enabled; email from internal with Contact in To or CC | Exclude Internal enabled. Email from internal team with one Contact in To or CC. | Exclusion rule is applied correctly (internal excluded or per product rule). Behavior and counts as designed. |
| 31 | Multiple Accounts/Opportunities; select None for Account | Contact with multiple related Accounts and Opportunities. In match popup select None for Account. | EmailMessage is logged only to the Contact. No Account or Opportunity link. |
| 32 | Multiple Accounts/Opportunities; select None for Opportunity | Contact with multiple related Accounts and Opportunities. In match popup select None for Opportunity. | EmailMessage is logged to Contact and Account only. No Opportunity link. |
| 33 | Exclude Marketing enabled | Exclude Marketing turned on. Try to import an email classified as marketing. | Email stays excluded. Toast: "1 email excluded by exclusion rules. No emails imported". Excluded count correct. |
| 34 | Send items - Contact in BCC | Sent email with Contact(s) in BCC and Contact(s) in To. | Only Contact(s) in To get matched and imported. Contacts in BCC are matched but not imported (no EmailMessage for BCC). |
| 35 | Inbox - Our email in BCC | Received email where our user is in BCC; To/CC include matching Contact(s). | Email is imported and EmailMessage is logged under the matching Contact(s). |
| 36 | Exclude Public domains enabled | Exclude Public domains turned on. Inbox has email from a configured public domain. | Public domain email is skipped. Excluded count correct. No EmailMessage for that email. |

How to use:
- "Needed for testing" = data, setup, and steps required to run the scenario.
- "Expected from code" = pass criteria; the system should behave as described for the test to pass.
